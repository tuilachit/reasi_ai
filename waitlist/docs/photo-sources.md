Images are editorial illustrations of everyday life, not patients, clinicians,
testimonials or clinical-outcome evidence. The scenes do not imply that Reasi
caused a particular activity or health result. Attribution is also available in
the page footer. Downloaded source images are preserved and displayed in their
natural colour, with CSS crops for each layout. The optional dark theme adjusts
image brightness without removing colour.

The photographs provide the main colour against the white page and black
typography. Sage, peach and sky reflections in gradient glass cards complement
them. Glass overlays retain their readable tint and blur without a monochrome
filter. Photo wrappers add small scroll-linked movement within the crop;
reduced-motion preferences disable that movement.
